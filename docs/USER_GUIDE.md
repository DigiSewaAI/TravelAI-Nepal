Emily, a graphic designer from Australia, had always wanted to see the Himalayas. One evening, a friend sent her a photo of Poon Hill at sunrise. She decided: *"I'm going to Nepal this year."*

But where to start? Which trek? How many days? What permits? What would it cost?

Another friend said: *"Try TravelAI Nepal — it's an AI-powered platform for planning Himalayan treks."*

Emily opened the TravelAI Nepal website on her laptop.

Emily clicked the **Destination** field. A searchable dropdown appeared with **138+ destinations** — everything from "Annapurna Base Camp Trek" to "Zip-lining in Pokhara."

- Annapurna Base Camp Trek
- Annapurna Circuit Trek

She selected: **Annapurna Base Camp Trek**

**💡 Tip:** The **Number of Days** field **auto-filled to 9** — because the platform knows the real trek duration from its database. She could change it if she wanted.
