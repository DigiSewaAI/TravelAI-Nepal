# 📊 TravelAI Nepal: Comprehensive Business Module Coverage

**Vision:** To digitize and empower Nepal's tourism industry by providing a tailored, AI-driven digital infrastructure for every type of travel and hospitality business.

### 🎯 The Core Strategy: 18 Provider Types
We have meticulously engineered **18 distinct Provider Types**. This architecture ensures that **over 95% of Nepal's tourism ecosystem** is seamlessly covered. Whether a business is a small local homestay or a large trekking agency, our platform provides a customized dashboard tailored to their specific operational needs.

### 💻 8 Dedicated Dashboards (Dynamic UI)
Unlike generic platforms, TravelAI Nepal uses a **Vertical SaaS model**. A Restaurant Owner doesn't need to see "Room Inventory," and a Trekking Agency doesn't need "Currency Exchange Rates." 

We have built **8 unique dashboards** that automatically adapt to the provider's business type:

1. 🟨 **Hotel Dashboard** (Gold Theme)
2. 🟦 **Trek & Tour Base Dashboard** (Blue Theme)
3. 🟪 **Inventory Dashboard** (Indigo Theme)
4. 🟩 **Individual Services Dashboard** (Emerald Theme)
5. 🟧 **Restaurant Dashboard** (Orange Theme)
6. 🌐 **Transport Dashboard** (Sky Blue Theme)
7. 💱 **Money Changer Dashboard** (FinTech Green Theme)
8. 🎨 **Art/Craft Dashboard** (Terracotta Theme)
